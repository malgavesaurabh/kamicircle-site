What KAMI Circle is, and isn’t

KAMI Circle brings together data from the devices you connect (such as Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch and your phone) and compares each day with your own normal. Scores such as readiness and Kinetic Load are estimates for general wellness and motivation.

KAMI Circle is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Don’t use it to make medical decisions. If you feel unwell or have a health concern, talk to a doctor. Exercise at your own risk and listen to your body, whatever a score says.
