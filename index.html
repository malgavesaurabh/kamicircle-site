KAMI Circle

Your circle. Any device. Fair for every body.

KAMI Circle brings your friends’ health together, whatever they wear. See how everyone slept, recovered and moved, each compared with their own normal.

KAMI stands for Keeper of All Metrics and Insights. Private beta for friends, on iPhone and Android.

What it does

Any device

Whoop, Oura, Apple Watch or just your phone. Everyone fits in one circle.

Kinetic Load

One effort score across every device. Your tank starts full each morning and drains with every workout and step.

Fair for every body

Everyone is measured against their own normal, not someone else’s.

Better together

A feed, group chat, cheers and Stepathons with your friends.

Your data, your call

For each metric you choose what your circle sees: exact numbers, just the trend, or nothing. We never sell your data or show ads. Read the Privacy Policy.
